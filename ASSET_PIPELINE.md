# SAN AURELIO - ASSET PIPELINE & PROCEDURAL SYNTHESIS

---

## 1. Asset Strategy & Zero External Asset Dependency
To achieve instantaneous boot times in any browser without gigabytes of external downloads or CORS failures, the San Aurelio engine utilizes a high-efficiency **procedural geometric synthesis and shader pipeline** conforming strictly to the 360 Design Blueprints:

1. **Character Synthesis (Kai Mercer / Mira Kade):**
   - High-mobility humanoid rig with articulated torso, head, shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, and feet.
   - Distinct asymmetrical tactical jacket with high-collar trim, modular utility harness, cargo pockets, tactical boots.
   - Sockets for primary rifle back sling, sidearm thigh holster, and active hand grips.
   - Multi-phase walk, jog, sprint, jump, climb, and vehicle seated blend animations.

2. **Vehicle Synthesis (10 Distinct Architectural Classes):**
   - **Sports Coupe (VX-9 Kestrel):** Aerodynamic low-profile wedge chassis, rear diffuser, glowing taillights, responsive dual exhaust.
   - **Sedan (Aurelia Regent):** Balanced executive profile, 4 passenger doors, chrome grille.
   - **Utility Pickup (Redwood 250):** Elevated suspension, open cargo bed, rugged all-terrain wheels.
   - **Panel Van (Courier L4):** High-top delivery body, rear cargo doors, commercial signage.
   - **Compact (Mica Hatch):** Urban runabout, tight wheelbase, nimble collision radius.
   - **Motorbike (Kite 600):** Dual-wheel fork chassis, exposed engine block, dynamic leaning rig.
   - **Speed Boat (TideRunner 24):** Hydrodynamic deep-V hull, dual outboard motors, wake spray emitter.
   - **Helicopter (HX-4 Sparrow):** Rotor hub with spinning main blades, tail boom rotor, skids, searchlight beam.
   - **Light Tank (AR-7 Mastiff):** Dual heavy track assemblies, armored chassis, 360-degree rotating turret, elevated cannon barrel.
   - **Emergency Cruiser (AMPS Interceptor):** Aerodynamic cruiser chassis with red/blue high-intensity strobe lightbar.

3. **Modular District Architecture:**
   - Meridian Financial Towers: Multi-story glass curtain facades, recessed plazas, illuminated penthouse crests.
   - Old Quay Waterfront: Stucco and brick facades, arched windows, terracotta tiled roofs.
   - Ironworks Industrial: Corrugated sheet metal warehouses, structural steel trusses, smoking exhaust stacks.
   - Suburban & Coastal: Rendered dwellings, palm tree boulevards, beachfront boardwalks.
