## 1. Setting Overview
San Aurelio is a sun-bleached coastal metropolis and regional economic hub situated in the Federal Republic of Vesper. It features stark contrasts: glass-clad financial skyscrapers in Meridian Core, cobblestones and historic archways in Old Quarter / Old Quay, sprawling shipping container yards in South Docks and Ironworks, sun-soaked beaches along Saltwater Coast, and towering arid foothills leading up into the Caldera Range and Blackridge Reserve.

### Core Lore & Atmosphere
- **Nation:** Federal Republic of Vesper
- **Province:** Aurelio Province
- **Primary Currency:** Aurelians (AUR, ₳)
- **Law Enforcement:** Aurelio Metropolitan Police Service (AMPS)
- **Transit Authority:** Aurelio Metro & Transit (AMT)
- **Atmosphere:** High-contrast coastal light, cinematic synth-funk and ambient industrial soundscapes, vibrant district-specific street cultures, underground syndicate tensions.

## 3. Characters

### Protagonist: Kai Mercer (Operational Alias: Mira Kade)
- **Role:** Agile urban operator and syndicate freelance specialist.
- **Visual Silhouette:** Athleisure layered utility jacket with high collar, fitted technical cargo trousers, reinforced street trainers, utility wrist comms, tactical fingerless gloves.
- **Philosophy:** Ruthlessly pragmatic, calculating, non-flashy, master of traversal, driving, and tactical deterrence.

### Fictional Agencies & Factions
- **AMPS:** Aurelio Metropolitan Police Service. Crisp navy and white cruisers, strict heat escalation protocol.
- **Kestrel Transport & Courier:** Legitimate logistics firm used for covert cross-city asset moving.
- **Vesper Steel & Forge:** Industrial manufacturing union dominating Ironworks and Port Meridian.
- **Aurelio Syndicate:** Underworld broker network delivering contract missions via encrypted burner terminals.
